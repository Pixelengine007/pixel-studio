1 — Silhouette dusk

Black shapes against glowing, layered skies, with fog and light rays. Moody and cinematic (Limbo, Badland, Alto). Very cheap to animate and always looks expensive.

2 — Low-poly 3D

Real 3D built from flat-shaded triangles, with soft shadows and a toy-diorama feel (Monument Valley, Townscaper). The camera can move, which is great for clips.

3 — Neon glow

Glowing lines on deep night colours: arcade energy, high contrast, pops out of an Instagram feed (Geometry Wars, Tron, synthwave).

4 — Papercraft

Cut-paper layers with real paper grain and shadows between the layers. Warm, handmade, cozy (Tearaway, Paper Mario).
